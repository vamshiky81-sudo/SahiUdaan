🛂 Visa Guide for Indian Passport Holders
🇦🇪 UAE (Dubai)
Visa on Arrival
30 days free for Indian passport holders. No pre-application. Just show up.
🇹🇭 Thailand
Visa on Arrival
30 days on arrival. Fee: ₹1,900 approx. Easy process at airport.
🇸🇬 Singapore
Visa on Arrival
30 days on arrival for Indians. No fee. Simple immigration card to fill.
🇮🇩 Bali, Indonesia
Visa on Arrival
30 days on arrival. Fee: USD 35 (~₹2,900). Very popular for Indians.
🇦🇺 Australia
e-Visa (Online)
Apply online in 24-48 hrs. AUD 20 (~₹1,100). ETA visa for Indians.
🇬🇧 United Kingdom
Visa Required
UK Standard Visitor Visa. Apply at VFS Global. Takes 3-6 weeks. Fee: ~₹12,000.
🇺🇸 USA
Visa Required
B1/B2 Tourist Visa. Embassy appointment required. Wait time: 2-12 months.
🇫🇷 Europe (Schengen)
Visa Required
Schengen Visa covers 26 European countries. Apply at VFS. Takes 15-30 days.
🇰🇪 Kenya
e-Visa (Online)
Apply at evisa.go.ke. USD 51 (~₹4,200). Approval in 2-3 days.
